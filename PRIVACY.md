# Privacy policy

Earshot is a podcast app with no account, no analytics and no advertising. Hartmann Studios collects nothing
about you and runs no servers for the app.

## What stays on your device

The shows you follow, the episodes you've played and where you are in each, your Up next list, your settings and
any episodes you download are kept in the app's own folder on your device. Removing the app with its data
removes them.

## Who Earshot talks to

- **The shows you follow.** Earshot downloads each show's feed, artwork and episodes from wherever the show
  publishes them. Like any podcast app, this means the show's host can see your IP address and that the request
  came from Earshot.
- **The Apple Podcasts directory**, only when you open Discover or search. It receives the words you search
  for, the chart you're looking at and your region (taken from your device's language settings). It does not
  receive anything about the shows you follow or what you listen to.

Nothing else. Earshot sends no usage data, crash reports or identifiers anywhere.

## Your files

Earshot reads a subscriptions file only when you pick one to import, and writes one only where you choose to
export it.
